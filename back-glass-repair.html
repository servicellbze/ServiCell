When to replace the back glass

  • Cracks or spider-webbing across the back
  • Sharp edges or glass flaking off
  • Chips around the camera area
  • Gaps where dust or moisture can get in

What the repair includes

  • We remove the broken back glass with specialised equipment
  • We fit new back glass and reseal the phone
  • We check the phone works properly before you pick it up
  • Parts we supply come with a warranty of at least 3 days from pickup

Questions about back glass repair

Do I need to replace the whole phone frame?

Not usually. We replace the cracked glass panel itself. Send us a photo on WhatsApp and we'll tell you what your phone needs.

Will my phone still be water-resistant?

We reseal the phone with industrial adhesive, but opening a factory-sealed phone breaks the original seal, so we can't guarantee the original water-resistance rating.

Should I put a case on it afterwards?

We'd recommend it. We stock cases, screen protectors and lens covers to help keep your phone safe.
