Questions about software & virus removal

Will I lose my data?

Some fixes can be done without erasing anything, but others need a full reset. We'll explain your options before we do anything that could erase data.

Is it a software or hardware problem?

Sometimes it's hard to tell. We'll diagnose the phone first and tell you what we find before any work is done.

Do you work on both Android and iPhone?

Yes, we handle software problems on both Android and iOS.
