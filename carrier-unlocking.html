Phone Carrier Unlocking in Belize City

Bought a phone that only works with one carrier? We provide factory unlocks so you can use a SIM card from the network you choose, whether you're switching providers in Belize or travelling.

Your phone may be carrier-locked if

  • It shows "SIM not supported", "SIM not valid" or asks for an unlock code with a new SIM
  • It was bought from a carrier or imported from abroad
  • Calls and data only work with one provider's SIM

How it works

  • Tell us your phone model and the carrier it's locked to
  • We check which unlock option fits your phone
  • We apply a factory unlock so the phone accepts other SIM cards
  • We test it with a different SIM before you go

Find your phone's IMEI number by dialling *#06#. It helps us check your phone before you visit.

Questions about carrier unlocking

Which phones can you unlock?

It depends on the model and the carrier it's locked to. Send us the model and carrier on WhatsApp and we'll confirm before you come in.

Is a factory unlock permanent?

A factory unlock is normally permanent and stays in place after software updates.

What information do you need?

Your phone model, the carrier it's locked to, and the IMEI number (dial *#06# to see it).
