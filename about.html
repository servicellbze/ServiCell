Our Story

Founded in 2014 by a single passionate technician, ServiCell began with a simple mission: to fix electronics reliably without breaking the bank. What started as a personal hobby has since grown into a tight-knit team of 2–3 dedicated technicians. At ServiCell, we believe every device deserves a second chance, and every customer deserves transparency, care, and respect.

Why Trust ServiCell?

  • Experienced Technicians: Our tight-knit team has been repairing phones in Belize City since 2014, so your Samsung or iPhone is in capable hands.
  • Affordable & Transparent: Honest estimates and competitive pricing. You know exactly what you're paying for.
  • Fast & Reliable: We aim to repair your devices quickly—often same-day—without sacrificing quality.
  • Trust & Experience: Over a decade of building a reputation in Belize City based on reliability.
