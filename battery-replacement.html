Signs your battery needs replacing

  • The battery drains much faster than it used to
  • The phone shuts off suddenly while still showing charge
  • It only works while plugged in
  • The iPhone battery health shows "Service" or a low maximum capacity
  • The battery is swollen, pushing up the screen or back cover

What the repair includes

  • We remove the old battery safely
  • We fit the new battery and reseal the phone
  • We test charging and power-on before you pick it up
  • Parts we supply come with a warranty of at least 3 days from pickup

Questions about battery replacement

How do I check my battery health?

On iPhone, go to Settings › Battery › Battery Health & Charging. On Samsung, open Settings › Battery and device care, or run a battery test in the Samsung Members app. Not sure? Bring it in and we'll take a look.

How long does a battery replacement take?

Many battery replacements are done the same day, depending on your model and whether the battery is in stock. We'll give you an estimate at drop-off.

Will I lose my data?

A battery replacement doesn't normally affect your data, but please back up your phone before drop-off just in case.

Is a swollen battery dangerous?

Yes, it can be. Stop charging the phone, don't press on the bulge, and bring it to us as soon as possible.
