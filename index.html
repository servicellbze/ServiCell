FAQ

Common questions

How long does a repair take?

Many repairs are done the same day. Bigger jobs, or repairs that need a part ordered, take longer. We'll give you an estimate at drop-off, and you can follow your repair live on our Repair Tracker.

Which phones do you repair?

We specialise in iPhone and Samsung: screens, batteries, back glass, charging ports and software issues. Have a different brand? Message us on WhatsApp and we'll let you know.

Is there a warranty on repairs?

Yes. Parts supplied by ServiCell come with a short-term warranty of at least 3 days from pickup, and some repairs get longer. If something isn't right within that window, bring it back and we'll fix it at no extra cost. See our service policy.

Can I bring my own part?

Yes, we'll install parts you bought elsewhere and charge labour only. We can't guarantee the part itself, and if it's faulty a second labour fee applies to swap it. For full peace of mind, buy the part from us.

What should I do before dropping off my phone?

Back up your data (iCloud or Google), charge it to at least 20%, remove your SIM and SD cards, and remove or share your passcode so we can test the repair.

Will my phone still be water-resistant after a repair?

We reseal phones with industrial adhesive, but opening a factory-sealed phone breaks the original seal, so we can't guarantee the original water-resistance rating.

How long will you hold my phone after it's ready?

Please collect within 90 days. After 90 days, devices are considered abandoned so we can recover repair costs. We'll let you know as soon as it's ready.
